Frequently Asked Questions About Partnering With Evo Hawks

How do your sprint contracts work?

We operate on milestone-based sprint contracts. Every engagement starts with a defined scope, clear deliverables, and scheduled sign-offs (typically 50% mobilization deposit and 50% upon final milestone approval).

Are ad budgets included in your agency fees?

No. Advertising spend on platforms like Meta Ads and Google Ads is billed directly by those platforms to your company card. Our agency fees cover creative production, campaign architecture, A/B testing, and weekly optimization.

Do I own all code and creative assets?

Yes, 100%. We provide full asset handover upon final milestone payment. You retain full intellectual property ownership of every line of code, design file, raw video export, and ad account asset.
