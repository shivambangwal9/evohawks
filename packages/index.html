Scope Boundaries & Advertising Budget Separation

  • Agency Fees vs Advertising Spend: Ad spend budgets on Meta Ads and Google Ads are paid directly to the ad platforms by the client and are strictly separate from agency production fees.

  • Infrastructure Costs: Domain registrations, specialized hosting, and third-party software subscriptions are client responsibilities.
