Solaris Kinetic Architecture: Architectural Design Portfolio

Architectural portfolio website with kinetic typography, smooth canvas animations, and global press inquiry integration.

Verified Benchmark: Fluid 60fps canvas animation rendering with responsive Retina image optimization.

Our Strategic Solution
